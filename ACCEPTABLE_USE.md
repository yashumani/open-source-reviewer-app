# ForkWise Acceptable Use Policy

ForkWise is intended for responsible, evidence-based review of public open-source repositories.

You must not use ForkWise to:

- submit private, stolen, confidential, personal, or regulated data;
- expose credentials, secrets, exploit details, or vulnerability reports in public channels;
- evade GitHub, ForkWise, or hosting-provider rate limits and access controls;
- conduct abusive, deceptive, unlawful, harassing, or discriminatory activity;
- overload the service through automated bulk submission without prior approval;
- misrepresent a ForkWise report as a security certification, legal opinion, or project endorsement;
- use findings to harass maintainers or contributors;
- attempt to cause ForkWise to execute repository-controlled code;
- interfere with service availability, integrity, or other users.

Responsible research and good-faith testing are welcome when they preserve the static-only boundary and follow `SECURITY.md`.

The project may limit or block activity that threatens safety, availability, or community participation.
